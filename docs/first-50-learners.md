# First 50 learners experiment

Goal: bring 50 real Algebra 1 learners through the measurable funnel before major curriculum expansion.

Track weekly:
- Visitors and diagnostic starts
- Diagnostic completion rate
- Account creation rate
- Lesson activation rate
- Trial exhaustion rate
- Upgrade click rate
- Checkout start rate
- Paid conversion rate
- 7-day active learner rate

Decision rule: fix the largest measurable funnel drop-off before adding broad new product scope.
