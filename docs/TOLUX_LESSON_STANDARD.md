# Tolux Lesson Standard

This is the standing instructional standard for every Algebra 1 lesson, retrofit, and future module in Tolux AI Math Coach.

## 1. Teach visually when the mathematics can be seen
- Include graphs, mapping diagrams, tables, number lines, algebra layouts, transformation sketches, factor/X-method layouts, or other topic-appropriate visuals.
- Visuals must teach the mathematical idea, not decorate the page.
- Use responsive layouts that work on desktop and phone.

## 2. Comprehensive help must be available throughout learning
- **I'm Stuck** uses progressive, topic-specific hints.
- Hint 1 identifies the first useful idea.
- Hint 2 develops the reasoning.
- Hint 3 gives the correct answer and complete reasoning.
- Avoid generic fallback text when a topic-specific explanation can be supplied.

## 3. Explain Another Way must actually reteach
- Provide a different representation, strategy, graph interpretation, paper method, or conceptual route.
- Include complete step-by-step reasoning.
- Show the final answer.

## 4. Reveal the correct answer after a student attempts
- In guided, independent, similar-problem, remediation, and practice learning interactions, once a student submits an incorrect checked attempt, Tolux must make the correct answer and full worked explanation readily available.
- The student should never be left without a way to compare their work with the correct mathematics.
- Mastery checks may preserve independent scoring during the five-item check, but the review/remediation immediately afterward must reveal correct answers and reasoning.

## 5. Correct answers should also teach
- A correct response should show why it is correct when that explanation is useful for learning.
- Do not show only “Correct” when the full reasoning would materially help the student.

## 6. Mathematical notation must be classroom-standard
- Render exponents as superscripts where practical (for example, x² rather than x^2 in displayed instruction).
- Use standard radical, inequality, interval, function, coordinate, and algebra notation.
- Input may accept keyboard-friendly equivalents, but student-facing instruction should display conventional mathematical notation.
- Audit every new lesson for notation before QA approval.

## 7. Independent-learning expectation
Every lesson should support this complete loop:
1. Learn the concept.
2. See a visual or concrete representation when appropriate.
3. Watch complete worked examples.
4. Complete guided practice.
5. Use I'm Stuck, Explain Another Way, and Similar Problem without losing the current task.
6. Complete independent practice.
7. See the correct answer and full solution after a checked attempt when correction is needed.
8. Complete mastery.
9. Receive targeted remediation with correct answers, explanations, and a recheck.
10. Save progress.

## 8. QA approval checklist
A lesson is not ready for owner approval unless:
- visual teaching is present where appropriate;
- help is topic-specific and sufficiently detailed;
- Hint 3 reveals answer + reasoning;
- Explain Another Way is complete and different;
- incorrect checked attempts can reveal answer + full solution;
- mathematical notation is correct;
- correct answers are graded robustly, including reasonable equivalent notation;
- Similar Problem works and remains scoped to the current skill;
- progress and navigation complete cleanly without dead ends.
