Help & Support

Having trouble, want to share your experience, or need help with your subscription? Contact Tolux at info@tolux.org. You do not need to sign in to ask for help.

Send a support request

This form prepares an email in your email app. You must send it there; nothing is submitted or stored by this page.

Do not include passwords, payment card numbers, or sensitive student information. If helpful, attach a screenshot in your email app after removing personal details.

If your email app does not open, email info@tolux.org directly with your request.

Billing and cancellation

Signed-in subscribers can securely open Stripe to view the billing options enabled for their subscription. These can include invoices, payment-method updates, and cancellation. Stripe confirms any account change before it takes effect.

Sign in with the email used to subscribe, then return here.

If Stripe cannot find the subscription or does not offer the change you need, choose “Billing question” or “Cancel my subscription” above and include the email used to subscribe. Sending an email request does not itself cancel billing; ask for confirmation and the effective date. Signing out or stopping use of the app does not cancel billing.

Refund requests

Choose “Refund request,” include your account email, the charge date, and the reason for your request. Requests are reviewed individually; this page does not issue or guarantee a refund.

Report a math issue or share feedback

For a grading issue, include the lesson or test name, question, your answer, and the result shown. For feedback or a complaint, tell us what worked, what did not, and how we can improve.
