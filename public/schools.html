Why Tolux

Built to teach the method, not just provide an answer.

Tolux is designed around the learning cycle educators already care about: identify the gap, teach the concept, guide practice, check understanding, remediate, and demonstrate mastery.

Readiness before remediation

Students can begin with a diagnostic experience that helps surface prerequisite gaps before they move into instruction and practice.

Structured teaching

Tutor Mode is designed to teach concepts step by step with examples, guided support, and clear mathematical notation rather than acting as a general answer bot.

Evidence of progress

Lesson and practice activity can be used to build a clearer picture of what a student attempted, completed, and is working toward mastering.

What the pilot includes

A focused classroom evaluation—not a long procurement commitment.

The goal is to let your team evaluate instructional fit with a manageable group before discussing any broader implementation.

1

15-minute planning call

We identify the Algebra 1 class, intervention group, or student cohort you want to test with and agree on a simple success measure.

2

Teacher and student setup

We provide the pilot workflow and orient the educator to the readiness, tutoring, practice, and progress experience.

3

2–4 week classroom trial

Students use Tolux during the pilot period while the educator observes engagement, quality of explanations, practice usefulness, and mastery progression.

4

End-of-pilot review

We review what worked, what needs improvement, and whether a larger teacher, campus, tutoring-center, or district pilot makes sense.
